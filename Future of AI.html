The History of AI:

1950: The Turing Test

A challenge to test a machine's intelligence and see if it can imitate a human. This test was founded by Alan Turing.

1956: The Birth of AI

A group of scientists came together and discussed the current state of AI. They sparked future ideas and set a goal to better understand human intelligence.

1966: The First Chatbot

The first chatbot, ELIZA, was developed in 1966 by Joseph Weizenbaum. It was designed to imitate a therapist.

1997: Deep Blue vs. Kasparov

Historic chess match that saw IBM's supercomputer "Deep Blue" defeat the world chess champion Gary Kasparov.

2011: Siri is Introduced

Apple took the world by storm with its launch of its AI-wired Voice Assistant, Siri.

2018: Self-driving Cars

Self-driving cars officially hit the road in 2018 with the launch of Waymo's self-driving taxi service.

2020: The AI Revolution

When ChatGPT launched, its capabilities shocked the world. This chatbot collects all sorts of data from the internet and gives human-like responses.
